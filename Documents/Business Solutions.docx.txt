Business Solutions
At Workforce Career Center (WCC), we take a hands-on, partnership-driven approach to working with employers. Our team collaborates closely with businesses to understand their workforce needs, operational challenges, and long-term hiring goals. Using this insight, we design customized training and recruitment solutions that connect companies with skilled, job-ready talent.
Through WCC’s innovative “Mall of Schools” workforce model, multiple training providers operate within our facility to deliver industry-specific programs aligned with employer demand. This ecosystem allows businesses to access a pipeline of candidates trained in fields such as construction trades, healthcare, technology, logistics, and other high-growth industries.
Our programs are designed to balance the workforce equation—supporting both job seekers gaining valuable skills and employers seeking qualified workers who can contribute immediately.
Competitive Workforce Solutions
At Workforce Career Center, we prepare and connect talent that enables businesses to adapt quickly to changing market demands while maintaining productivity and operational efficiency.
Our employer partnerships allow companies to participate in the training process, ensuring graduates meet industry expectations and are prepared for real-world job environments.
Work-Based Learning Opportunities
WCC works with employers and community partners to create meaningful work-based learning opportunities that help individuals transition from training into sustainable careers.
These opportunities include:
Apprenticeships
On-the-Job Training (OJT)
Paid Work Experience Programs
Internship and Pre-Apprenticeship Programs
Employer-sponsored training pathways
We specialize in supporting individuals who may face barriers to employment—including veterans, returning citizens, individuals with disabilities, and underserved populations—while helping businesses build strong and inclusive workforces.
Proven Recruitment and Hiring Strategies
Workforce Career Center provides businesses with recruitment and workforce development services designed to streamline hiring and improve employee retention.
Our services include:
Direct candidate placement
Customized workforce training programs
Recruitment events and hiring fairs
Workforce consulting and talent pipeline development
Upskilling and incumbent worker training
By working directly with employers, our team ensures candidates are prepared not only with technical skills but also with the professional behaviors and work readiness required for long-term success.
Benefits to Employers
Partnering with Workforce Career Center provides businesses with:
Reduced recruitment and onboarding costs
Access to a diverse pipeline of trained and certified candidates
Improved employee retention through work-based learning pathways
Opportunities to leverage workforce development grants, tax incentives, and training subsidies
Direct engagement with training providers to shape curriculum and workforce pipelines
