Who We Are

Workforce Career Center (WCC) is an innovative workforce development hub where multiple training providers and industry experts come together under one roof to deliver high-quality career training and certification programs.

Rather than operating as a traditional school, WCC serves as a collaborative training ecosystem that brings together specialized schools, instructors, and workforce partners to offer a wide range of in-demand career pathways. From skilled trades and healthcare to technology and emerging industries, our facilities provide the space, infrastructure, and support services that allow trusted training providers to educate and certify the next generation of workers.

Through strong partnerships with employers, workforce agencies, and community organizations, WCC ensures that training programs are aligned with real labor market demand. By expanding access to education, removing barriers to training, and connecting students directly to employment opportunities, we help individuals launch sustainable careers while strengthening the workforce in the communities we serve.

Together with our partners, WCC is redefining workforce training by creating a centralized destination where opportunity, education, and career advancement meet.

About Ticket to Work

The Ticket to Work and Self Sufficiency (Ticket) Program is a Federally-funded employment program designed to provide Social Security disability beneficiaries the choices, opportunities and support they need to enter the workforce and maintain employment with the goal of becoming economically self-supporting over time.

This section of the website provides basic facts about the Ticket to Work Program, including some historical information and information on the Ticket to Work Program partners at the national, state and local levels.

Pre-Employment Transition Services

Pre-Employment Services (Pre-ETS) can be provided to both potentially eligible students or VR eligible customers. The five (5) Pre-ETS required under WIOA that Georgia Vocational Rehabilitation Agency (GVRA) provides are:

  • explore Job exploration counseling
  • school Counseling on post-secondary opportunities
  • engineering Workplace readiness training
  • work Work-based learning
  • record_voice_over Self-advocacy instruction

Transition services are more intensive, individualized services provided under an Individualized Plan for Employment (IPE). The IPE is developed with the student and outlines intermediate objectives, funding sources, partners, and resources necessary to support a successful transition into competitive integrated employment.

Eligibility

A student is eligible for Pre-ETS if they are at least 14 years old and have not yet turned 22, and are enrolled in an educational program with an Individualized Education Plan (IEP), 504 plan, or other disability documentation.
